Pizza

Pizza (Italian: (Italian: ['pittsa], Neapolitan: ['pittsa]) is a salvory dish of Italian origin with toa usually round, flatted base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients (such as anchovies, mushroom, onions, olives, pineapple, meat, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven.[1] A small pizza is sometimes called a pizzeta. A preson who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.
